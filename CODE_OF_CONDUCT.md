# Code of conduct

PRISM is built by people with different backgrounds, experience levels and ways of working. We
want taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be constructive: explain the problem, suggest a way forward, and accept that the project may
  choose differently.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Give credit, and accept feedback on your own work gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public channels unless privacy is needed.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone else to harm or harass.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews or events.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where you
represent the project.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you or someone else uncomfortable, contact the maintainers
privately through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or through GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting if you need a private channel on this repository. Reports are
handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and respond in proportion: a private note, a public
reminder, a temporary ban from participating, or a permanent ban for serious or repeated
violations. Maintainers who don't follow this code in good faith may face the same consequences.
